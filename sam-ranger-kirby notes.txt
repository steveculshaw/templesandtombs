
Born 1895 in San Antonio, Texas

Dad (Wayne Kirby), blacksmith then mechanic 
Ma (Alice Kirby, nee Baker)
Meemaw (Jenny Baker) and Papaw William Baker emigrated to America in 1850's from London East End

Dad took me to see the first flight in Houston, Texas in February 1910
I was hooked 

He had a contract with the army for work at Fort Sam Houston (NE San Antonio)
And in 1913 started working on 1st Aero Squadron engines ... Burgess and Curtis 
I helped with the engines, and got to begging flights off the officers

Pilot in WW1, flew a Sopworth Camel 

Last combat was against German ace, Baron von Tripoff ... shot each other down
Saved his life, and we became good friends.
